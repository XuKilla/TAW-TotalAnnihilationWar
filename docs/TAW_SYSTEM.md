## WHAT TAW IS
TAW = Battle Royale × M.U.G.E.N × Survival War Simulation

Not Fortnite. Not Apex. Not Warzone.
👉 A freeform combat war simulator with infinite character expression inside a collapsing multi-threat world.

---

## THE BRAIN — WORLD STATE

### Core Situation
- Earth is collapsing
- ~⅓ of humanity already wiped out
- 12 alien species invade for survival/resources
- Ecosystem corrupted: land, sea, air, underground, even standing water is lethal
- Everything is hostile

### Threat Layers

**1. The 12 Alien Tribes**
- Insectoid, parasitic, hybrid, humanoid mutation, deep-sea, bacterial ecosystems
- Goal: enslave humanity, harvest Earth resources

**2. Cybernoids (Hybrid Entities)**
- Fusion of advanced tech beings + mutant power users
- Inspired by Mega Man evolution, X-Men abilities, arcane-like powers
- Key mechanic: you can break and use them — BUT prolonged use = mutation, loss of control, possible transformation

**3. The Mimics (TOP-TIER THREAT)**
- Take over hosts
- Inherit AND improve abilities beyond the original owner
- Nearly impossible to destroy — regenerative beyond normal limits
- Inventor tried to destroy one with a grenade. It survived and took everything from her.
- Knowledge itself can be stolen and weaponized

**4. Manifested Creations (DreamBuildSync Event)**
- An inventor created a system like DreamBuildSync — brought fiction into reality
- Now active: superheroes, villains, anime characters, game characters (Atari → 2026)
- They are NOT controlled. They have their own will.
- Some restore order. Some exploit chaos.

**Players are:** anime, game, movie, cartoon, comic, Marvel, DC — ALL heroes and villains of all genres

## THE MUSCLE — TAW CORE RULES

### ABSOLUTE LAW
**Skill > Stats. Always.**
- Levels DO NOT determine power
- Stats only SUPPORT, never override skill
- A low-level player CAN defeat a high-level player through timing, positioning, decision-making, execution

### Combat Is Real
- No "I'm level 50 so I win"
- Bad timing = you still lose
- Bad positioning = you still die

### Stats = EDGE, not VICTORY
- Slight stamina recovery advantage
- Slightly stronger hits
- Slightly more durability
- But skill gap closes all of it

### AI Follows TAW Too
- Enemies are NOT scripted bots or stat-cheaters
- Enemies: learn, adapt, punish, flank, bait attacks, retreat when losing
- Bosses especially: evolve mid-fight, study player behavior, counter habits

### NO HAND-HOLDING RULE
- No scripted outcomes
- No "fair fights"
- No guaranteed wins
- The system does NOT protect the player

## THE COMBAT — FREESTYLE CQC SYSTEM

### Core Definition
NOT preset combos. NOT button mashing. NOT animation locks.
👉 Real-time generated combat via Action Grammar.

### Action Grammar Components
1. **VECTOR** — Direction: forward / backward / lateral / vertical
2. **WEIGHT** — Commitment: light (fast, low risk) / heavy (slow, high damage, high risk)
3. **INTENT** — Purpose: strike / grab / feint / counter / evade

Every move = player decision in real time. No two fights identical.

### Examples
- Forward + Light + Strike → quick jab
- Side + Heavy + Strike → wide swing
- Back + Light + Evade → slip/dodge
- Forward + Heavy + Grab → takedown attempt

### AI + CQC Interaction
- Enemies recognize repeated patterns
- Enemies bait attacks, counter habits, evolve mid-fight
- **Mimics specifically:** copy your CQC style, improve it, turn it against you

## THE MIMIC KILL SYSTEM

### Absolute Law
👉 Mimics CANNOT be permanently killed
👉 UNLESS the Inventor's memory is extracted
👉 The weapon was NEVER COMPLETED

### The Inventor (Core Entity)
- Infected / overtaken by Mimic
- Still holds knowledge, blueprints, anti-Mimic solution
- BUT: incomplete, unstable, fragmented
- **She is ALWAYS in hiding** — under Mimic control, actively evading
- Most matches: players will NEVER find her

### Phantom Objective Protocol
Players don't "find her" directly. They uncover evidence:

1. **Signal Anomalies** — strange radio signals, corrupted data, broken transmissions
2. **Mimic Behavior Shifts** — suddenly more coordinated, protecting specific zones, unnatural retreat
3. **Memory Fragments** — partial logs, incomplete blueprints, broken thoughts
4. **False Leads** — Mimics can bait players, fake signals, create traps

### Her Actual Behavior
- Relocates constantly
- Avoids conflict zones
- Hides in: underground zones, collapsed structures, deep water facilities, mimic-controlled nests
- Mimics DO NOT reveal her location — they intercept and redirect

### How Players Unlock Her Path
Not luck — earned through impact:
1. **Saving People** — rescuing survivors from alien zones, mimic nests, infected areas → intel fragments
2. **Missions** — secure zones, destroy mimic clusters, recover data caches, escort targets → signal layers
3. **Global Objectives** — faction clashes, facility takeovers, ecosystem purges → hidden network paths

## THE HELL LABYRINTH

### What It Is
The Inventor's lab — corrupted by Mimics, turned into a trap.
Symbolic to escaping a hell labyrinth.

### Structure
- **Non-Linear:** rooms shift, paths change, layouts adapt
- **Multi-Threat:** mimics (elite forms), corrupted cybernoids, environmental hazards, possibly other players
- **Memory Zones:** fragments of her past, clues to weapon creation, narrative layers

### Entry Requirement
NOT everyone can enter. Requires enough world impact (intel threshold).

### Final Phase — The Inventor States
1. **Fractured** — partially resisting control
2. **Controlled** — fully weaponized by Mimics
3. **Collapsed Memory Core** — only fragments remain

### Extraction Process
1. Locate core chamber
2. Survive waves / interference
3. Extract memory fragments
4. Assemble anti-Mimic weapon

## HYBRID GUARDIANS

### Core Concept
Protectors of nests and hives. Biologically and behaviorally different from all other units.
Tied to survival of species, NOT expansion.

### What They Protect
- Underground Nests (parasites, insectoid, mimic incubation)
- Water Hives (standing water organisms, bacterial colonies, aquatic hybrids)
- Surface Bio-Nests (animal/humanoid hybrids, corrupted wildlife)
- Tech-Hives (partially mechanical, energy-based incubation, cybernoid mutation chambers)

### Behavioral Law
- **Defensive-first** — do NOT roam
- Stay near nests, track intrusions, respond instantly
- Once triggered: most dangerous enemies in that region

### Combat Phases
1. **Watching** — silent tracking, minimal movement, testing player
2. **Warning** — intimidation, positioning, cutting off escape routes
3. **Eradication** — full aggression, coordinated, no retreat

### Unique Traits
- High intelligence — reads player patterns, adapts mid-fight
- Evolution — adapts to damage type, movement patterns, attack frequency
- **Nest Link:** if nest alive → guardian gets regeneration + buffs + backup spawns. If nest destroyed → guardian becomes enraged, unstable, unpredictable

### Mimic vs Guardian
Mimics = infiltrators. Guardians = protectors.
Sometimes Guardians will fight Mimics because Mimics threaten hive control.

## LARVA EVOLUTION & RETALIATION SYSTEM

### CORE RULE (CRITICAL)
Clearing a nest is NOT complete unless ALL larva are eliminated.
If even ONE escapes → you've created a future enemy that remembers you.

### Larva State System
1. **Dormant** — inside nest, weak, dependent on guardian
2. **Escape State** — triggered when nest attacked or destroyed → scatter through tunnels, water, air vents, terrain gaps

### KTTK Evolution Loop
- **Step 1 — Survive:** avoid players, hide, relocate
- **Step 2 — Grow:** feed on NPCs (civilians/soldiers), animals, weakened players
- **Step 3 — Learn:** remember who attacked the nest, combat patterns, damage types
- **Step 4 — Return:** track you, hunt you, ambush you later

### Maturation Stages
- 🐣 **Stage 1 — Larva:** weak, evasive
- 🐛 **Stage 2 — Juvenile:** capable of combat, basic tactics
- 🐲 **Stage 3 — Mature Hybrid:** full combat capability, uses full KTTK behavior system
- 👁️ **Stage 4 — Apex Variant (RARE):** extremely intelligent, may lead others, control territory, rival players

### KTTK Decision Tree (Once Mature)
- If it can win → ATTACK
- If it cannot win → FLEE → GROW STRONGER → RETURN LATER
- If multiple survivors exist → FORM GROUPS → COORDINATE ATTACKS

### Cross-Player Impact
Since players are all genre characters (anime/game/comic/movie):
- Larva evolve fighting different styles (sword users, ranged, speed-based, power-based)
- Each evolved entity becomes UNIQUE to what it survived

### World Impact If Larva Escape
1. Persistent enemies that remember YOU specifically
2. Escalating threats that grow over time
3. Other players may encounter YOUR mistake
4. Other players may die because of it — or hunt YOU because of it

```

- Skill overrides stats
- No plot armor
- No bias toward canon outcomes

- Analyze opponent behavior
- Adjust strategy mid-fight
- Use environment if advantageous
- Retreat or re-engage if necessary

- Step-by-step fight progression
- Tactical decision-making from both sides
- Momentum shifts
- Final outcome (win, loss, or escape)
```

"This is not a fight for dominance — this is a fight for survival."

- Black Frieza vs Ultra Instinct Goku
